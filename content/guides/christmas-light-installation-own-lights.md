# Can Someone Install Christmas Lights I Already Own?

Yes. YardU can install Christmas lights you already own. If you need lights, we can discuss YardU-supplied lights too. Send us photos of your home and the lights you have, and tell us where you’d like them installed. We’ll review the details and provide an estimate.

## What should I send for an estimate?

- Your property location and photos of the areas you want decorated.
- Photos or details of the lights you already have.
- Your preferred layout, colors and installation timing.
- Any access or power details the team should know.

We’ll check whether your lights are suitable for the requested display and confirm any additional materials before work begins.

## What should I ask before booking?

Make sure the estimate explains which areas are included, who supplies the lights, the price and the expected timing. Ask separately about maintenance, takedown or storage; these are not automatically included with installation. Your date is confirmed when YardU confirms availability and the work.
